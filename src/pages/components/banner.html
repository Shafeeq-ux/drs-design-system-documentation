Banner

A compact strip with an icon and a short, neutral message, shown across the top of a page or section.

2 Figma variants.aec-banner

Variants

This vehicle is in transit to your dealer.

Estimated arrival: Nov 12

We'll email you when it's ready for pickup.

0% APR for 60 months on select 2025 models.

Offer ends Dec 2.

From 768px up, the title and description sit on one line. On mobile they stack, and the padding drops to 12/16px.

Usage

  • Banners are neutral and have no status colours. For success, warning or error, use an alert.
  • Show at most one banner per page region, and keep the message to one line on desktop.

Accessibility

Landmark WCAG 1.3.1

A site-wide banner can be wrapped in <aside aria-label="Announcement">. If it's added after the page loads, give it role="status".

Figma notes

  • Banner has an 8px radius, while Alert and Tooltip are square and Snackbar is 4px. Consider aligning these.
  • The border style is set but has a stroke weight of 0. The page also holds loose Avenir font-specimen layers.
