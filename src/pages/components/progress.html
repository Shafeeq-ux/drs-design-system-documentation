Progress Beta

Shows that something is happening and, when known, how far along it is, for example uploading documents or checking credit.

Spinner · linear.aec-spinner · .aec-progress

Not yet designed. The Figma Progress page only holds a placeholder (four grey 24px circles). These styles are a token-based proposal for the design team to review and may change.

Use a spinner for short waits of unknown length and a linear bar when you can show how much is done. Progress has no status colours (success, warning, error, info) and no brand component tokens of its own. It uses the semantic colour tokens --aec-color-primary-default, --aec-color-primary-subtle and --aec-color-bg-variant, so it still re-themes with the brand. When a task finishes or fails, replace the indicator with a message, such as an alert or a snackbar.

Variants

Spinner

Three sizes: .aec-spinner--sm, default and .aec-spinner--lg. Use small inside compact elements, large for a whole panel.

Linear, determinate

Shows how much is done. Set --aec-progress and aria-valuenow.

Linear, large

.aec-progress--lg. A thicker bar for a page-level task, such as submitting a credit application.

Linear, indeterminate

.aec-progress--indeterminate. A moving segment for a step that has a clear place on the page but no known length.

Spinner

For short waits of unknown length, such as loading offers.

Linear

For tasks with a known end point, such as a document upload. Set the value with --aec-progress and aria-valuenow. Wrap the bar in .aec-progress-field to add a label and value.

Usage

Content

  • Label: say what is happening with an -ing verb, for example Uploading insurance card or Checking credit. Sentence case, no full stop, no ellipsis.
  • Value: show a percentage only when it's accurate. Don't let it jump back or sit at 99%.
  • When it's done: change the label to the result, such as Pay stub uploaded, or replace the indicator with a message. If it fails, show an error alert that says what to do next.
  • Avoid "Please wait" and "Loading…" on their own. Say what is loading.

Accessibility

Role and name WCAG 4.1.2

Use role="progressbar" with a label and aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax. Leave the values out for indeterminate progress. Spinners use role="status" with an aria-label.

Motion WCAG 2.3.3

Animations slow down when prefers-reduced-motion is set.

Announcing the result WCAG 4.1.3

Screen readers don't announce every value change. When the task ends, announce the result in a role="status" region, for example with a snackbar.

Busy regions

Set aria-busy="true" on the area that is loading and remove it when the content arrives.

Contrast WCAG 1.4.3 · 1.4.11

Label text needs 4.5:1. The indicator is a graphic people need to understand progress, so it needs 3:1 against the track, and the spinner arc needs 3:1 against the page. These ratios are measured live in the active brand. The bars are samples of each colour.
