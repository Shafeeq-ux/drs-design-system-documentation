Alert

Displays an important message about the current page or task. It stays visible until the issue is resolved or the person dismisses it.

4 statuses.aec-alert

Use an alert for messages tied to the content in front of the user, such as Your credit application was approved. Alerts come in four statuses: success, warning, error and info. For brief confirmations of an action, use a snackbar. For neutral announcements at the top of a page, use a banner. For a hint about a single term, use a tooltip.

Statuses

Choose the status by what the message means for the person, not by how serious it looks. Each status has its own colour, icon and accent border, all set by the brand's notification tokens (--aec-notification-color-<status>-*).

Success

Something finished as expected, for example a pre-qualification or a verified document.

Your trade-in value is locked for 7 days.

Warning

Something needs attention soon but doesn't block progress, such as an offer that is about to end.

Your reservation expires in 24 hours.

Error

Something went wrong and the person can't continue until they fix it.

We couldn't verify your insurance. Upload a new card.

Info

Useful context that helps people decide, such as what a price includes.

Prices exclude tax, title and registration.

You're pre-qualified

Estimated APR 5.9% for 60 months. Your lender confirms the final terms.

Offer ends soon

The $1,500 loyalty bonus expires on October 31.

We couldn't verify your address

Check the ZIP code and try again.

Edit address

Prices include destination freight but exclude tax, title and registration.

Optional parts

The icon and message are always shown. The title, action link and close button are optional. Use .aec-alert--flat to remove the shadow when the alert sits inside a card or panel.

Message only. Add a title when the message needs more than one sentence.

Your payment didn't go through

Your card was declined. Try another card or contact your bank.

Change payment method

Flat alert

No shadow, and it fills the width of its container.

Interactive states

The alert itself isn't interactive. Its action link and close button are, and they keep their own states inside every status. Focus adds the 2px focus/default ring.

Usage

Alerts interrupt. Use them sparingly, and only when the message changes what the person should do next.

Content

Say what happened, why it matters and what to do next, in that order. Write in plain English and sentence case.

  • Title: optional. Three to six words that sum up the message, for example Offer ends soon. No full stop.
  • Message: one or two short sentences. Lead with the fact, then the next step. Use full stops.
  • Action: a link that starts with a verb and names the fix, such as Edit address. Use one action at most.
  • Don't blame the person ("You entered an invalid ZIP"). Describe the problem ("We couldn't find that ZIP code").

Accessibility

Announcements WCAG 4.1.3

Use role="alert" only for urgent errors that appear dynamically. Use role="status" for success, info and warning. Alerts present when the page loads need neither.

Not colour alone WCAG 1.4.1

Each status has its own icon and message, so meaning doesn't depend on the accent colour. The icon is decorative (aria-hidden="true"), so the words must carry the meaning.

Dismissing WCAG 2.1.1

The close button is a labelled icon button. When the alert is removed, focus moves to the next element.

Link text WCAG 2.4.4

The action link must make sense out of context, because screen reader users often list the links on a page.

Text contrast WCAG 1.4.3

Title and message text need at least 4.5:1 against the alert background. These ratios are measured live in the active brand.

Figma notes

  • Every status uses the same placeholder icon. The code uses status icons from the library (state-success, warning, state-error, info).
  • Information uses grey #666666 in Figma. The brand token --aec-notification-color-information-icon follows Figma (grey in DRS Base), but the semantic info/default colour is blue. Confirm which is intended.
  • The shadow is a raw effect, but its values match shadow/m exactly.
