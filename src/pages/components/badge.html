Badge

Highlights a count, or shows a notification dot, on another element such as an icon, tab, avatar or chip.

9 Figma variants.aec-badge

A badge is not interactive. It decorates another element. To label something with a word, such as New or Pending, use an information chip.

Variants

Three colours, each as a dot or a count. The colours come from the brand's badge tokens (--aec-badge-color-primary-*, -black-* and -white-*).

Primary

The default. Uses the brand's primary colour.

312

Dark

A neutral alternative to primary, for places where the primary colour would compete with nearby content.

312

Light

For dark or coloured surfaces only. It has no border.

312

States

Badges have no states of their own. They sit on a component that does, such as an icon button or a chip, and keep the same colours when it is hovered, focused or pressed. When the count changes, update the number in place.

Placement

Wrap the element and the badge in .aec-badge-anchor to pin the badge to the top-right corner. A ring in the page background colour separates it from the element underneath. Inside a chip or toggle, put the badge after the label instead.

4 AM

Usage

Content

A badge holds a number, or nothing at all. Use a dot when the presence of something matters more than how many there are.

Accessibility

Convey the meaning in text WCAG 1.1.1 · 1.3.1

A bare number isn't meaningful when read out on its own. Put the meaning in the parent control's name, such as aria-label="Notifications, 4 new", and hide the badge. Alternatively, add visually hidden text inside the badge.

Live counts WCAG 4.1.3

If a count changes while the page is open, announce the change through a polite live region.

Not colour alone WCAG 1.4.1

A dot only says "something is new". Make sure the parent's name or nearby text says what.

Not focusable

Badges never take focus or respond to clicks. Keyboard users reach the information through the parent control.

Text contrast WCAG 1.4.3

The count needs at least 4.5:1 against the badge. These ratios are measured live in the active brand.
