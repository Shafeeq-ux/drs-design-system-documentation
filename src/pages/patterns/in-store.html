FAQs

Figma notes

  • The currency is inconsistent (₤ and € in a US file), and "What is PCP?" is UK finance wording. These are template leftovers, so the code uses $ and neutral copy.
  • The sample vehicles are Acura and Hyundai models.
  • Raw colours with no token: #141414 and #3c3c3c (gallery header), #d9d9d9, #040404 at 50% (help video), and #bfbfc1 (VSR separator).
  • The vehicle details header separators use text/inverse, which makes them invisible on a light background.
