Accessibility

Keyboard WCAG 2.1.1

The arrow keys move by day or week, Page Up/Down by month, and Home/End to the start or end of the week. Enter or Space selects a day. Only one day is in the tab order.

Names WCAG 4.1.2

Each day button's name is the full date ("Thursday, November 12, 2026") and it uses aria-selected. Today has aria-current="date". The month title is announced when it changes.
