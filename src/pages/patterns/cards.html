  1. Media.aec-card__media: a product image (3-column 244×141 or 2-column 379×213) or a 40px icon. Vehicle and product photos are image slots.
  2. Chips.aec-card__chips, top right: dark overlay chips such as Upload proof, ID required or Conflicting offer.
  3. BodyBody-1 bold title, body-2 text/subtle description, styled list or bullets, and a small link CTA.
  4. Price detail.aec-card-price: Price, Price & total, Month & miles, or a loading skeleton.
  5. Card buttonA toggle button that switches between "Add" and "Added". Alternatives are a Free tag, a spinner, or "Contact dealer".

The shell is bg/default with a 1px border/default border, square corners and no shadow. Padding is 16px. The footer divider is inset 16px.

Responsive

Put cards in .aec-card-grid. When the grid is narrower than 560px, product cards switch to the mobile layout automatically, with a 128px media column on the left. This uses a container query, so it works in narrow columns as well as on phones.

Accessibility

Structure WCAG 1.3.1

Each card is an <article> with a heading. Lists of cards are grids of articles, not one big link.

Add / Added WCAG 4.1.2

The card button is a toggle with aria-pressed. Add data-select-card to also highlight the card. If several cards share the label "Add", connect each button to its card title with aria-describedby.

Overlay chips WCAG 1.4.3

White text on backdrop/default over a photo stays readable on any image.

Figma notes

  • Cards use radius 0 almost everywhere. The exceptions are payment method (new), payment summary and credit application (8px) and upload image (4px).
  • The selected border is inconsistent: rebates, chargers and subscriptions turn action/bg/default, while plans, packages and accessories keep border/default. The code highlights every selected card.
  • Card chips are the information chip in Figma, but they render as dark overlays. The code adds .aec-chip--overlay.
