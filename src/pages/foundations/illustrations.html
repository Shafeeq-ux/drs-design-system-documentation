Foundations

Illustrations

Spot illustrations add warmth to empty states, confirmations and feature highlights. They use the decoration palette.

Library

Guidance

  • Use one illustration per view, next to a heading that explains it.
  • Illustrations are decorative: give them alt="" unless they carry information the text doesn't.
  • Don't recolour or crop illustrations, and don't use them in place of icons.
