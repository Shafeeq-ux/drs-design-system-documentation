Foundations

Colour

Colour tokens are named by role, not by hue. Use the role that describes what an element does, such as text, a border or a primary action, and the right value follows.

83 tokensPrefix --aec-color-

Every token maps one-to-one to a Figma colour style. The style primary/default becomes --aec-color-primary-default. Click a swatch to copy its var().

Contrast is measured against white, or against text/default if that scores higher. AA means at least 4.5:1, which is safe for body text.

Brand

Chevrolet blue is the primary colour for links, focus and the main call to action. Secondary is near-black and used for strong, neutral emphasis.

Action

Fills and borders for interactive controls such as buttons, chips, toggles and selectable cards. Use action/inverse on dark surfaces.

Action – inverse

Status

Feedback for success, warning, error and information. Pair a subtle background with its on-subtle text, and a default background with its on-default text.

warning/default (#b3842d) is only 3.36:1 against white. Use it for icons, borders and large text, and keep body text in text/default. Text on a warning/default fill uses warning/on-default (#1a1a1a).

Inverse

For dark surfaces such as the header, footer, tooltips and snackbars.

Greys and decoration

Supporting greys, plus decoration colours for illustrations and promotional accents. Don't use decoration colours to convey status.
