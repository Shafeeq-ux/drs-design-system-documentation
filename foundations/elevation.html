Elevation

Shadows show how far a surface sits above the page. The higher the surface, the larger and softer the shadow.

Guidance

  • Use elevation to show layering, such as a menu over content or a modal over the page. Don't use it for decoration.
  • Increase elevation by one level on hover for clickable cards (for example s → m).
  • Pair modals with --aec-color-backdrop-default behind them.
