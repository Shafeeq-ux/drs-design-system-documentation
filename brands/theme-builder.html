Hand off to developers

  1. Choose Full brand and download <file name>.css. For a new brand, this file goes in tokens/brands/source/ and is registered in tokens/brands/brands.json. See Add a brand.
  2. To change a few tokens in an existing brand, choose Changed tokens only and share the .vars.css or .json file, or copy the share link. The link opens this tool with your edits applied.
  3. Brand fonts are not included. The export lists the font family names. If you picked a Google font, its name is in the JSON under brand.fonts.

Your work is saved in this browser as a draft. The share link carries only your changes, compressed in the part of the URL after #, which is never sent to a server.
