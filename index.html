About

The DRS (Digital Retail Secure checkout) design system brings together the components, styles and design decisions used across the vehicle purchase flow: vehicle selection, trade-in, financing, protection plans and delivery. A shared source of truth streamlines design work, improves collaboration between design and engineering, and keeps every screen consistent and accessible.

Use the brand menu in the header to switch every page between DRS Base, Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, Cadillac and Car Bravo. Start with Foundations to learn the tokens, then explore the components. Use Brand tokens to download a brand.
