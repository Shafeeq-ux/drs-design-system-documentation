  • The header is 56px tall at every breakpoint. On screens wider than 1440 it stays full width while the page is centred.
  • The content has 24px top padding (16px on mobile). Its bottom padding clears the sticky 64px bottom bar.
  • Children of .aec-layout__content span the full width by default. .aec-col-half spans half the grid (full width on mobile) and .aec-col-third a third (full width below 1280). Use grid-column: span n for anything else.

Figma notes

  • The left nav is 216px in some templates (1440, 1920) and 238px in others (1600, 1280, 1024). The code uses 216px at ≥1280 and 238px at 1024–1279, matching the most common frames. Consider standardising on one width.
  • The 32px tablet margin has no spacing token.
  • The 375/360 templates have no navigation entry point or bottom bar. Those come from the page header and footers patterns.
