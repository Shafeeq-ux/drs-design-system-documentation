Stepper

Shows progress through a multi-step section, such as the trade-in or credit application, with a dot stepper and a "Step x/y" label.

Dots · segmented bar.aec-stepper · .aec-progress-steps

Dot stepper

Completed steps are filled success/default dots, the current step is an outlined green dot, and future steps are grey outlines. Connector lines turn green once the step before them is done. It works for any number of steps.

Segmented progress

A full-width bar of equal 4px segments. It's built into the bottom navigation bar to show overall checkout progress.

Accessibility

Text alternative WCAG 1.1.1

The dots are decorative. Give the group role="img" and an aria-label such as "Step 2 of 4".

Not colour alone WCAG 1.4.1

The visible "Step 2/4" label shows progress in text as well as colour.

Figma notes

  • The stepper is an unpublished base part with fixed 2, 3 and 4-step variants. Progress is set by overriding individual dots. The code builds any number of steps.
  • The labels don't always match the dots, and "Mobile Step 2" says "1/3" (typo).
  • Two progress indicators exist: green dots, and blue segments in the bottom bar. Consider keeping only one.
