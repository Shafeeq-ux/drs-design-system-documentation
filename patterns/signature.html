Signature sheet

The e-signing step of checkout. It has three full-screen screens: create a signature, accept the electronic signature disclosure, and sign each contract document.

Signing · Add Sign · Disclosure.aec-sign

1. Create your signature

The shopper types their full name and initials, then draws, picks a style or uploads a signature.

2. Electronic signature disclosure

A document preview with an acknowledgement footer. The primary action stays disabled until the box is checked.

3. Signing

Documents are listed on the left with each party's status, the document is in the middle with its signature fields, and the footer shows overall progress. Click a "Sign here" field to sign it.

Accessibility

Drawing alternative WCAG 2.5.7 · 2.1.1

Drawing needs a pointer, so always offer a keyboard-friendly alternative ("Choose a style" or typed name). Don't make drawing the only option.

Signature fields WCAG 4.1.2

Each field is a button whose name says what is signed and by whom, such as "Sign here: buyer signature". The signature count updates in a polite live region.

Consent WCAG 3.3.4

Legal commitments need an explicit checkbox and a chance to review before submitting.
