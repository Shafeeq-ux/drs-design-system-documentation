Accessibility

Autocomplete WCAG 1.3.5

Card fields use cc-name, cc-number, cc-exp and cc-csc, so browsers and password managers can fill them.

Results WCAG 4.1.3

Success and authorised results are role="status", and failures are role="alert". Move focus to the result heading after submitting.

Remove buttons

Name each remove button after its card ("Remove card ending 4612").
