Accessibility

Carousel WCAG 4.1.2

Label the arrows ("Previous photo") and announce the position ("1 of 32") in a polite live region. Vehicle photos need meaningful alt text, such as "Front three-quarter view, Summit White".

Lightbox WCAG 2.1.2

Open the gallery as a modal dialog. Esc closes it and ← and → change the photo.

Figma notes
