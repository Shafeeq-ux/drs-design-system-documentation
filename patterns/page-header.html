Accessibility

Headings WCAG 1.3.1 · 2.4.6

The step title is the page's main heading (h1), or h2 when a welcome header sits above it. The back link names its destination.

Stepper text WCAG 1.1.1

Give the dot stepper role="img" with aria-label="Step 2 of 4".
