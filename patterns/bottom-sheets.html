Accessibility

Dialog WCAG 2.4.3

Bottom sheets are modal dialogs, so focus is trapped and Esc or the close button returns focus to the field.

Year grid WCAG 4.1.2

The grid is a radio group: the arrow keys move between years and the selection updates the field.
