Vehicle selector

Identifies a trade-in vehicle in three steps (year, then make, then model) in a panel under the "Make and model" field. On mobile it opens as a bottom sheet.

Accessibility

Step announcements WCAG 4.1.3

The breadcrumb line ("2023 › Chevrolet") is a polite live region, so each step change is announced.

Alternative input

Always offer VIN or licence-plate lookup as an alternative to browsing.
