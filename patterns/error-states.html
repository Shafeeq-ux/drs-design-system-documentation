Error & empty states

Fills a section when there's nothing to show, something failed to load, or a submission succeeded. It has an illustration, a short message and one or two actions.

Default · Success · Error.aec-empty

Variants

Add accessories to personalise your vehicle.

Content

  • Say what happened and what to do next ("Please try again"). Don't blame the user.
  • Use a primary action for the way forward and a standard action for alternatives.
  • Illustrations are decorative. Give them alt="".
