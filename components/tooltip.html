Tooltip

Shows a short hint or explanation when someone hovers over or focuses an element, for example what Destination freight includes.

Dark and light · 9 placements.aec-tooltip

A tooltip adds a little extra detail. It's never the only place important information appears, because touch-screen users often can't open it and many people never try. Tooltips come in two styles, dark and light, each with an optional title. They have no status colours: for a status message, use an alert.

Placement

Set data-placement to top (the default), bottom, left or right. Add -start or -end to top and bottom to align the tooltip to the trigger's edge. data-arrow="none" removes the pointer. Choose the placement that keeps the tooltip on screen and doesn't cover what the person is reading.

Behaviour

A tooltip has two states: hidden and shown. It shows when the trigger is hovered or receives keyboard focus, stays open while the pointer is over the tooltip, and hides on mouse-out, blur or Esc. The trigger keeps its own hover, focus and pressed states, documented on its page (for example icon button).

Usage

Content

  • Description: one or two short sentences. Explain, don't repeat. Use a full stop when it's a sentence.
  • Title: optional. Use it only when the tooltip groups a few facts, such as the offers applied to a price. Two or three words, no full stop.
  • Icon-button names: one or two words, starting with a verb where possible, such as Share or Edit trade-in. No full stop.
  • Write in sentence case and plain English. Spell out terms such as APR the first time they appear on the page, not only in a tooltip.

Accessibility

Role and relationship WCAG 4.1.2

The tooltip has role="tooltip" and an id, and the trigger points to it with aria-describedby.

Hover and focus WCAG 1.4.13

The tooltip shows on hover and on keyboard focus, stays visible while the pointer is over it, and can be dismissed with Esc (components.js).

Focusable trigger WCAG 2.1.1

Only put tooltips on focusable elements, such as buttons and links, so keyboard users can reach them.

Touch screens

Hover doesn't exist on touch screens. Make sure the page still makes sense if the tooltip is never seen.

Text contrast WCAG 1.4.3

Tooltip text needs at least 4.5:1 against the tooltip. These ratios are measured live in the active brand.

Figma notes

  • The placement property is called Arrow in the dark set and pointer in the light set, with values in a different order. The code uses data-placement for both.
  • The shadow is a raw effect, but its values match shadow/l exactly.
  • The light tooltip has no border, so on white backgrounds it is separated only by its shadow. Border tokens exist (--aec-tooltip-color-*-border) but the component doesn't use them.
  • Code: the brand tooltip tokens override the padding and text size of .aec-tooltip--sm, so in most brands the small tooltip only differs in maximum width. The design team should decide whether a small size is needed.
